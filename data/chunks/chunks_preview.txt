About HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Growth
HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Growth is a Hybrid Mutual Fund Scheme launched by HDFC Mutual Fund. This scheme was made available to investors on 10 Dec 1999. Anil Bamboli is the Current Fund Manager of HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Growth fund. The fund currently has an Asset Under Management(AUM) of ₹9,86,237 Cr and the Latest NAV as of 28 Sep 2026 is ₹551.04. The HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Growth is rated Very High risk. Minimum SIP Investment is set to ₹100. Minimum Lumpsum Investment is ₹100. Exit Load for units in excess of 15% of the investment,1% will be charged for redemption within 1 year. ;
